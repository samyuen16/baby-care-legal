Baby Care – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

What we collect

  • Account (Online mode only): a username and password you choose. No email address or phone number is requested. A private placeholder email is generated internally for sign-in; no mail is ever sent to it.
  • Baby records you enter: feeds, diaper changes, sleep, stool details, baby profile (name or nickname, birth date), reminder settings and language choice.
  • Photos (optional): diaper photos you take or choose, used to track stool colour.

Offline mode

In Offline mode everything stays on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to any server.

Online mode and where data is stored

Your records and photos are stored in a private account on Supabase (our hosting provider and data processor). Access is restricted by row-level security so that only the people signed in to your household account can read or change your data. Data is encrypted in transit.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no tracking, and no analytics or third-party SDKs that collect data about you.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties, other than the Supabase hosting described above.
  • Reminders are local notifications created on your device; no push-notification service is used.
  • Face ID (if you turn it on) is handled entirely by iOS. The app never receives your biometric data.

Not medical advice

Baby Care is a record-keeping tool. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Consult a doctor about your baby's health.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, all records, all photos and settings from our servers. You can also export a backup first from Settings. Offline-mode data is removed when you delete the app.

Children

The app is for parents and carers. It is not directed at children, and the records are about the parent's own baby.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
