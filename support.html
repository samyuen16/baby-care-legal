FAQ

How do I delete my account and data?

Settings → Delete account (at the bottom). 設定 → 刪除帳戶。

I forgot my password.

Accounts use a username and password only, so passwords cannot be reset by email. Email us with your username and we will help. 如忘記密碼，請電郵聯絡我們。

Can two phones share the same records?

Yes. Choose Online mode and sign in with the same username on both phones. 選擇線上模式，並在兩部手機用同一用戶名登入。

Can I use the app without an account?

Yes. Choose Offline mode; data stays on your device. 選擇離線模式即可，資料只存放在裝置上。

How do I back up my records?

Settings → Export backup. 設定 → 導出備份。

Is this medical advice?

No. Baby Care is a log, not a medical device. Always consult a doctor if you are worried about your baby. 本 App 並非醫療器材，如有疑慮請諮詢醫生。
